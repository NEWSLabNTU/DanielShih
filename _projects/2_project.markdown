Team NTU RoboPAL has been participating in the RoboCup Standard Platform League ([SPL](http://spl.robocup.org/)) since 2009, and won the first place for Shield Challenge in 2018 and 3rd place in 2011.

Members contribute to NTU RoboPAL RoboCup team are highly interested in solving multi-robot research issues in terms of perception, behavior, and motion. Such issues are critical in real world environments. Participating RoboCup competitions provides us a good real world scenario, and lets us discover new challenging robotics topics and exchange experiences with outstanding researchers from other countries.
