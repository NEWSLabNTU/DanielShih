# 2025 年第二期補助計劃開放申請

為鼓勵國立台灣大學教職員生積極參與台灣大學，美國伊利諾大學與日本九州大學三校之交流活動，三校聯盟中心開放申請補助參與三校聯盟相關之交流活動。

本次補助參與由三校聯盟之夥伴學校(包含台灣大學，日本九州大學或美國伊利諾大學)舉辦於 2025年 至 2026 年七月底前 的合作研究，合作教學，或是短期訪問。

補助辦法與申請表格：[連結](https://forms.illinois.edu/sec/827692166)

# The second phase of the subsidy program in 2025 is open for applications.

In order to encourage faculty, students, and staffs at National Taiwan University to actively participate in collaboration activities between National Taiwan University, the University of Illinois in the United States, and Kyushu University in Japan, the Three-University Alliance Center is open to apply for subsidies for students to participate in exchange activities related to the three-university alliance.

This subsidy will support the tri-lateral collaboration for research, teaching and mobility program between July 1st, 2025, and July 31st, 2026.

Guideline and Application：[link](https://forms.illinois.edu/sec/827692166)

Open for Application: March 24th, 2025 to April 30th, 2025.
