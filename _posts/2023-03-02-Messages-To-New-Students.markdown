Congratulations to be adimitted to NTU. 

### Where to start
Please visit my website and watch the [video](https://youtu.be/GzkLTNMnmyk) to know our current research directions. 

### If you find our researches are interersting: 
If you are interested in joining us, please pick one of the two groups and check if you can meet the requirements listed below. 

* Autonomous Vehicles group: the students should be familiar with 
1. Operating Systems
2. Computer Networks
3. C and Python programming. Knowing RUST is a plus.
4. (Optional) Machine learning

* Smart Sensor Group: the students should be familiar with
1. Computer Vision
2. Digital Circuit
3. Neural Networks or Green Learning
4. (Optional) Embedded systems.

### Requirements to the students

Below are the minimal requirements to the students:

- The first-year students have to attend at least two weekly meetings: lab meeting and research group meeting. 
- The second-year students also need to attend bi-weekly one-on-one thesis meeting.

### Expectation from the professor

* Below are my expectations to the students.
1. The students need to be self-motivated in my lab. Hence, there is no schedule for the students to show up in the lab. It depends on how you manage your own time. Due to the limited space, we will not have one designated seat for every student. The lab space wlll be used for experiments and conducting research.
2. The requirements for the thesis are to meet the quality of conference/journal papers at Rank B or better. 

### Support to the students
* Below are the supports to the students:
1. The students will receive monthly financial supports from NSTC (國科會) and/or our industrial partners. The amount will depend on the participation of the students. It may range from 8,000 ~ 16,000 for master students.
2. Computing facilities: laptop, access to high-performance computers, etc. 
3. Scholarships from or internships at our industrial partners

### What to do next
If the above sounds good to you, welcome to join us. Please send me an email to confirm the availability.
