We are pleased to announce the Call for Participation for CIV Summer School 2026. 
### General Information:
- 📅 Dates: August 31 – September 4, 2026
- 📍 Venue: Phoenix Seagaia Resort, Miyazaki, Japan (Pacific coast)
- 🎓 Audience: PhD students, early-career researchers, and young industry professionals
- ✉️ Official: https://civ-summerschool.org/

### 【Program】
- Cooperative Perception and Motion Planning
- Implicit and Explicit Interaction
- Modeling and Data-Driven Methods (AI / Foundation Models)
- Confirmed lecturers include Prof. Christoph Stiller (KIT), Prof. Alexander Carballo (Gifu University / Autoware), Chi-Sheng (Daniel) Shih (National Taiwan University, Taiwan), and Ryohsuke Mitsudome (TIER IV / Autoware Foundation Lead)
- Data Hackathon with real-world cooperative driving datasets
- Interactive poster sessions
- Cultural and outdoor activities along the Pacific coast

### 【Fees】 (includes accommodation, meals, and all activities)
- ¥160,000 — Academic students (university-funded)
- ¥220,000 — Industrial students & others (company-funded students, post-docs, young professionals)
- Single-room upgrade: +¥60,000 (Academic only; default is shared 3-person suite or 4-person cottage)
- An Inclusivity program (fee waiver, based on merit and need) is also available

### 【Key Dates】
- Application form opens: May 22, 2026
- 1st application deadline: June 22, 2026 (24:00 CET = 3pm PST, June 22)
- Results: June 29, 2026
- June 29 – August 20, 2026: First-come, first-served if seats remain
- Application form closes: August 20, 2026
- Note: Only applications submitted through the official form will be considered
