NTU-KU-UIUC Trilateral Center Plaque unveiled, marking the milestone of trilateral academic exchange and collaboration between the three universities.

On February 19, Kyushu University’s President, Dr. Tatsuro Ishibashi, University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign’s Chancellor, Dr. Robert Jones, and a delegation from both universities paid a special visit to NTU to jointly sign the MOU and unveil a plaque for the Trilateral Center. They were hosted by President Wen-Chang Chen, Vice President for International Affairs Hsiao-Wei Yuan, and Deans from the Colleges of Medicine, Engineering, Agriculture, and Social Sciences. The Trilateral Center marks a new chapter for academic exchange and collaborations between the three institutions. We look forward to the exciting developments as NTU, Kyushu U, and UIUC work together to co-create global opportunities for students and faculty!
