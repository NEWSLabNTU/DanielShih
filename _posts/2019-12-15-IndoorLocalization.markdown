title: National Innovation Award for Indoor Localization
date:   2019-12-14 16:40:16
description: News on the Award for Indoor Localization
---

We started to work on indoor localization in 2015 when we found there
is no perfect solution. There are more than 100 start-up companies
providing indoor solutions. Not mention existing service providers
such as Google. Some of them underestimated the complexity of indoor
localization and many of them do not ignore the real needs from the
users and the overhead of building owners.

Our solution use a cutomerized antenna to transmit location
information via Bluetooth protocol to provide sufficient accuracy and
maximum compatibility. In addition, the computation on edge and user
devices are minimized to reduce energy consumption on Bluetooth tag to
prolong its battery life to one year. The indoor localization has been
deployed to National Taiwan Hospital YunLin branch to assist the
out-patients to navigate in the hospital and the staffs to manage the
medical equipments.

The system has been award for 2019 Innovation Award (國家新創獎).
Thanks for the great works from the team.
