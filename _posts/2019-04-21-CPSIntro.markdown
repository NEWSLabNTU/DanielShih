description: Talk to general public to introduce cyber-physical systems using autonomous bus as an example.
---

Cyber-Physical system aims at designing intelligent and autonomous
systems for the future. In this talk to the general audience, I used
the autonomous bus as an example to show the transition from automated
systems into autonomous systems.

智慧整合控制系統(Cyber-Physical system)目標在開發一個自主性系統。在這
個科創講堂的演講中，我使用了無人駕駛公車作為例子，說明如何經由一個自動化
系統轉換成自主性系統的交通工具，解決交通壅塞的問題，提升運輸系統的效率。
