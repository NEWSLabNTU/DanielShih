NTU RoboPAL team have participated RoboCUP Standard League Platform
since 2008 and won the First Place at Shield Challenge in
RoboCup 2018. The outcomes of this team lead to the development of
autonomous vehicle project in Taiwan and also swarm of autonomous
robots for smart factories. The Office of Internatioal Affairs at
National Taiwan University selected NTU RoboPAL team to participate
2020 promotion video shooting.
