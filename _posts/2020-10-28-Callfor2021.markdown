Call for Papers for Medical Image Processing:

- Information Processing in Medical Imaging (IPMI) 2021:
[Call for Papers](http://ipmi2021.org/cfp/) Due on December 1st, 2020, 
Conference Date: June 27th, 2021 at Bornholm, Denmark.


- MIDL 2021: [Call for Papers](https://2021.midl.io/covid-19.html) Due
on February 10th, 2021, Conference Date: July 7, 2021 at Lubeck, Germany.

- MICCAI 2021: [Call for Papers](https://miccai2021.org/en/CALLS.html)
Due on February 10th, 2021, Conference Date: September 27, 2021 at
Strasbourg, FRANCE.
