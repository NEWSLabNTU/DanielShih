# 2025 年第一期補助計劃開放申請

為鼓勵國立台灣大學在學學生積極參與台灣大學，美國伊利諾大學與日本九州大學三校之交流活動，三校聯盟中心開放申請補助學生參與三校聯盟相關之交流活動。

本次補助參與由三校聯盟之夥伴學校(包含台灣大學，日本九州大學或美國伊利諾大學)舉辦於 2025年二月一日 至 2025年五月三十一日 間的國際活動之會議，研討會，競賽，或是短期訪問。

# The first phase of the subsidy program in 2025 is open for applications.

In order to encourage students currently studying at National Taiwan University to actively participate in exchange activities between National Taiwan University, the University of Illinois in the United States, and Kyushu University in Japan, the Three-University Alliance Center is open to apply for subsidies for students to participate in exchange activities related to the three-university alliance.

This subsidy is used to participate in international activities such as conferences, seminars, competitions, or short-term visits organized by the partner schools of the three-university alliance (including National Taiwan University, Kyushu University in Japan, or the University of Illinois in the United States) between February 1, 2025, and May 31, 2025.
