Our paper, entitled "Self-supervised multi-LiDAR object view generation using single LiDAR", was nomated for the Best Paper Award at the 29th IEEE International Conference on Embedded and Real-Time Computing Systems and Applications ([RTCSA 2023](https://rtcsa2023.github.io/)) to be presented on August 30th, 2023 ([Prorgam](https://rtcsa2023.github.io/program/)) at Niigata, Japan. Congratulations to the excellent work.
