2nd International Workshop on Explainability of Real-time Systems and their Analysis will be collocated with the IEEE Real-Time Systems Symposium (RTSS 2023) in Taipei, Taiwan.

Prof. Chi-Sheng (Daniel) Shih and Dr. Bjorn Andersson (CMU) are co-chairing the workshop. The call-for-papers can be founded at this [site](https://sites.google.com/view/ersa23/home). You are encouraged to submit your work. See you in Taipei in December 2023.
