We are pleased to announce the Call for Participation for CIV Summer School 2026. 

The CIV Summer School 2026 will be hosted at Phoenix Seagaia Resort, Miyazaki, Japan (Pacific coast). PhD students, early-career researchers, and young industry professionals are encouraged to join the summer school. 

The program includes 
- Cooperative Perception and Motion Planning
- Implicit and Explicit Interaction
- Modeling and Data-Driven Methods (AI / Foundation Models)
- Data Hackathon with real-world cooperative driving datasets
- Interactive poster sessions
- Cultural and outdoor activities along the Pacific coast

---
 
Important dates
- Application form opens: May 22, 2026
- 1st application deadline: June 22, 2026 (24:00 CET = 3pm PST, June 22)
- Results: June 29, 2026
- June 29 – August 20, 2026: First-come, first-served if seats remain
- Application form closes: August 20, 2026
- Note: Only applications submitted through the official form will be considered
