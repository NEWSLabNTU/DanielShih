Starting in November 1st, 2024, I will also serve the Director of the Center of Artificial Intelligence and Advanced Robotics([AIRobo](http://ai.robo.ntu.edu.tw/)) Center at National Taiwan University. The mission of the center is to to establish an interdisciplinary center which combines application of AI on robotics, industrial manufacturing, biomedicine and cognitive science in National Taiwan university.
